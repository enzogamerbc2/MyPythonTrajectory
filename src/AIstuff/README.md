## What does "NeuralNetwork.py" do

The neural network takes four physical measurements of a flower as its input numbers.It processes those numbers through layers of weighted mathematical connections to detect structural patterns.



## What does "AiVoice.py" do

* The script sets up a vocal system to read text out loud through your computer speakers.
* It downloads a conversational brain called DialoGPT to think of realistic responses.
* It opens an endless loop in your terminal that waits for your typed text inputs.
* It remembers your chat history so the conversation flows naturally and makes sense.
* It processes your input, prints the AI reply, and speaks the answer out loud.
